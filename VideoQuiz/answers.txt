写作题
作⽂ 1：你校英⽂报“Opinion”栏⽬正在进⾏征稿，请你就未来⼤学⽣活的三个要素：学习（study）、睡眠（sleep）
和社交（socialize），按重要性排序，并阐述理由。
注意：
1. 写作词数应为 80 左右；
2. 请按如下格式在答题纸的相应位置作答。
【写作思路】写作思路：先明确将学习排在⾸位、睡眠次之、社交第三，再分别说明学习是⼤学核⼼任务、睡眠保障⾝⼼健康与
学习效率、社交是锦上添花。
【参考范⽂·不是唯⼀答案】
When it comes to university life, I rank study ﬁrst, sleep second and socializing third.
Study matters most because university is fundamentally a place for learning. Academic performance shapes our
future careers, so it deserves top priority. Sleep comes next, as good rest keeps us healthy and energetic; without it,
neither study nor social life can go well. Socializing, though valuable for making friends and building skills, should
come last, since it can be adjusted around the other two.
In short, a balanced university life begins with study, is supported by sleep, and is enriched by socializing.
