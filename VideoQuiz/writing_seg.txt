作⽂ 1：你校英⽂报“Opinion”栏⽬正在进⾏征稿，请你就未来⼤学⽣活的三个要素：学习（study）、睡眠（sleep）
和社交（socialize），按重要性排序，并阐述理由。注意：1. 写作词数应为 80 左右；2. 请按如下格式在答题纸的相
应位置作答。
参考范⽂：写作思路：先明确将学习排在⾸位、睡眠次之、社交第三，再分别说明三者对⼤学⽣活的重要性及排序
理由。
In my view, the three elements of future university life should be ranked as study, sleep and socializing.
Study comes ﬁrst because university is mainly a place for academic growth. Without hard work, we cannot gain
knowledge or prepare for our future careers. Sleep ranks second, as enough rest keeps us healthy and energetic,
which is the basis for effective learning. Socializing comes third. Although making friends is important, it should not
take priority over study and health.
Therefore, I believe a balanced university life begins with study, supported by good sleep and proper social activities.
