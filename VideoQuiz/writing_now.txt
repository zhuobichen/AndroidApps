作⽂ 2：你校英⽂报“Opinion”栏⽬正在进⾏征稿，请你就未来⼤学⽣活的三个要素：学习（study）、睡眠（sleep）
和社交（socialize），按重要性排序，并阐述理由。
注意：
1. 写作词数应为 80 左右；
2. 请按如下格式在答题纸的相应位置作答。
参考范⽂：写作思路：先明确将学习排在⾸位、睡眠次之、社交第三，再分别说明学习是⼤学核⼼任务、睡眠保障
⾝⼼健康与学习效率、社交是锦上添花⽽⾮根本。
When it comes to university life, I rank study ﬁrst, sleep second and socializing third.
Study deserves top priority because acquiring knowledge and skills is the core mission of university education and
lays the foundation for our future careers. Sleep comes next, as adequate rest keeps us healthy and sharpens our
minds, without which neither study nor social life can be sustained. Socializing, though valuable for building
friendships and communication skills, should not come at the expense of the other two.
In short, a balanced university life begins with academic commitment, supported by good rest, with social activities
as a rewarding addition.
