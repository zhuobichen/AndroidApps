写作题
作⽂ 1：你校英⽂报“Opinion”栏⽬正在进⾏征稿，请你就未来⼤学⽣活的三个要素：学习（study）、睡眠（sleep）
和社交（socialize），按重要性排序，并阐述理由。
注意：
1. 写作词数应为 80 左右；
2. 请按如下格式在答题纸的相应位置作答。
【写作思路】写作思路：先明确将学习排在⾸位、睡眠次之、社交第三，再分别说明理由，突出⼤学阶段学业为本、健康是基
础、社交需适度。
【参考范⽂·不是唯⼀答案】
When it comes to university life, I rank study ﬁrst, sleep second and socializing third.
Study matters most because university is above all a place for learning. Academic performance shapes our future
careers, so we should give it top priority. Sleep comes next, as good health is the foundation of everything. Without
enough rest, we cannot concentrate in class or enjoy campus life. Socializing, though important, should come last.
Making friends enriches our experience, but it should never take up the time needed for study and rest.
In short, a balanced university life begins with study, supported by sleep, and enriched by socializing.

【参考范⽂·不是唯⼀答案】
The couple suggested that Emily take a train to meet her boyfriend. After a moment of hesitation, she agreed, realizing
it was wiser than driving alone on such a long journey. She thanked them warmly and set off by train that afternoon.
Three days later, Emily came back to pick up her car. She looked relaxed and happy, her face glowing with joy. She told
the couple that the trip had been wonderful and that her boyfriend had met her at the station. Before leaving, she
hugged them and said their advice had made everything easier. The couple smiled, glad that their small suggestion
had brought her such happiness.
